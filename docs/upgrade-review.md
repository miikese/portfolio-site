## Remaining release boundaries

Private-source overviews remain private-source overviews. SmartConvert needs authorized live-provider integrations; Global Browser needs its real hosting/routing setup; ReachAid remains a controlled pilot requiring provider verification and operational checks. These prerequisites cannot be supplied by visual changes or simulated transactions. Early static prototypes are not promoted as full platforms.

Local previews run separately from production accounts and data. Passing a local or mocked browser check does not establish production deployment, successful payment settlement, public service availability or real customer adoption.
